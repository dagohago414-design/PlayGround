FIELD NOTES

How to explore

01
Move & look

WASD or the touch joystick to move. Drag the world to orbit the camera. Shift or a full joystick push to run.

02
Find wild creatures

Little creatures wander the meadow, pond, stone circle and hill. Walk close, then press A or tap Interact.

03
Battle & catch

Pick a move, whittle down HP, then throw a Wild Orb. Lower HP means a better catch. Wins earn more orbs.

04
Rest & collect

The campfire heals your whole team. Catch all 21 species to complete the Meadow Expedition.
